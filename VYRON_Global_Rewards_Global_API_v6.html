Member services

Help & Support

How VYRON Rewards works

Members create an account using a phone number and password. Phone ownership is confirmed through a one-time SMS code (OTP); Gmail is optional. Rewards are calculated from confirmed advertising revenue after fees and fraud checks.

Global payout policy

The payout route is determined by the member’s verified residence country, not by the display-currency selector. Mozambique uses the verified M-Pesa phone number. Other supported countries use international bank transfer through an approved payout API, including SWIFT where available. Unsupported countries or corridors cannot submit a withdrawal.

Automatic international bank payments

After a withdrawal request, the secure backend must re-check identity, country, bank-account ownership, balance, fees, exchange rate, sanctions and fraud risk. It then submits the payment through the contracted provider API, records the provider reference and updates the transaction status from webhook or status polling. Failed or returned transfers must be reconciled and the balance handled safely.

Withdrawals and payment security

In Mozambique, the default M-Pesa payout destination is the verified phone number linked to the VYRON account. Changing it requires fresh verification. VYRON must never ask for the user's M-Pesa PIN; payment authorization happens only through the official payment provider flow.

Fair access & anti-fraud

Using a VPN to pretend to be in another country will not increase rewards. Country eligibility and reward calculations must be verified on the server, not trusted from the browser.

VYRON is currently a frontend prototype. SMS OTP, secure authentication, live ads, wallet ledger, M-Pesa payments and international bank-transfer APIs are not connected. The full-screen video player currently demonstrates the interface only. The displayed 3% withdrawal fee is illustrative and not a finalized tariff.
