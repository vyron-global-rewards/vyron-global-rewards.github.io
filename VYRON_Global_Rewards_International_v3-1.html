Member services

Help & Support

How VYRON Rewards works

Members create an account using a phone number and password. Phone ownership is confirmed through a one-time SMS code (OTP); Gmail is optional. Rewards are calculated from confirmed advertising revenue after fees and fraud checks.

Global payout policy

VYRON is designed to show local currencies and supported payout methods by verified country. Examples include M-Pesa in Mozambique, bank transfer and supported e-wallets in other markets. Availability is not yet connected or guaranteed.

Withdrawals and payment security

In Mozambique, the default M-Pesa payout destination is the verified phone number linked to the VYRON account. Changing it requires fresh verification. VYRON must never ask for the user's M-Pesa PIN; payment authorization happens only through the official payment provider flow.

Fair access & anti-fraud

Using a VPN to pretend to be in another country will not increase rewards. Country eligibility and reward calculations must be verified on the server, not trusted from the browser.

VYRON is currently a frontend prototype. SMS OTP, secure authentication, live ads, real wallet and M-Pesa payments are not connected. The full-screen video player currently demonstrates the interface only. The displayed 3% withdrawal fee is illustrative and not a finalized tariff.
